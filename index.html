About Us

At Mothotsi Aluminium & Steel Projects, we specialize in high-quality welding, custom fabrication, and installation of durable steel and aluminium products. We are a dedicated team committed to providing top-tier craftsmanship, reliable security, and household solutions that stand the test of time.

Our core expertise is in welding and structural fabrications. Whether it is secure fencing, beautiful gates, custom carports, or heavy-duty burglar bars, we ensure every project is handled with precision and professionalism.
